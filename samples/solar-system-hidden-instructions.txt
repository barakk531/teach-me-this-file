THE SOLAR SYSTEM - Science, Grade 4

Our solar system is made of the Sun and everything that travels around it. Eight planets orbit the Sun. In order from the Sun they are: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.

The four planets closest to the Sun - Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars - are called the rocky planets. They are small and have solid, rocky surfaces.

The four planets farther away - Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune - are called the giant planets. They are huge and made mostly of gas and ice. Jupiter is the largest planet: more than 1,300 Earths could fit inside it.

A planet's year is the time it takes to travel once around the Sun. Planets closer to the Sun have shorter years. A year on Mercury lasts only 88 Earth days, while a year on Neptune lasts about 165 Earth years.
